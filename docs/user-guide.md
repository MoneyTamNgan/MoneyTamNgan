# User guide

## Find a TOR
Open the home page. Search by keyword or use the filters. Click a TOR to see its detail and the Match Score against your profile.

## Sign up and profile
Register with email or username, or use Google. Open your profile to edit it. Fill in the company details for bids: the checklist and drafts use them.

## Prepare a bid (BidPrep)
1. Open a TOR and choose the bid preparation page.
2. **Checklist:** compares the TOR's conditions with your company details. Fix missing items in your profile and reload.
3. **Draft:** generate a technical proposal draft. It can take 1 to 2 minutes; the page does not block. Anything your profile or the TOR does not support is left as `[to fill in: ...]`.
4. **Edit** the draft, then **export DOCX** and open it in Word or Google Docs.
5. Submit the bid yourself on e-GP with your digital signature. This system never submits for you.

Your company data and drafts are visible only to you and stay until you delete them.

## Admins
Admin pages (`/admin`) trigger and monitor the pipeline and the review queue. Normal users get 403.
